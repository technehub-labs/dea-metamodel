CR-10 — Scenario, Simulation, Digital Twin & Strategic Decision Intelligence

Status: Proposed
Depends on: CR-1 → CR-9
Purpose: Extend OpenDEA from a semantic/runtime platform into a decision-intelligence and enterprise change simulation platform.

CR-9 established the runtime loop:

Observe → Model → Assess → Reason → Decide → Act → Observe

CR-10 introduces the ability to ask:

“What if?”

and:

“What should we do?”

without changing the live enterprise state.

⸻

P. The most important architectural principle going forward

I would put this prominently in the repository:

OpenDEA is a semantic contract for representing, connecting, governing, assessing, reasoning about, and evolving an enterprise.

It is not limited to an architecture repository, visualization tool, knowledge graph, AI framework, assessment framework, or digital twin.

Those are implementation and application layers that consume and extend the OpenDEA semantic foundation.

And the resulting conceptual stack becomes:

                         OPENDEA
                            │
        ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
        │                   │                   │
     REPRESENT            UNDERSTAND          EVOLVE
        │                   │                   │
     Metamodel          Knowledge Graph      Scenarios
     Profiles           Assessment           Simulation
     Relationships      Reasoning            Decisions
     State              Evidence             Transformation
        │                   │                   │
        └───────────────────┼───────────────────┘
                            │
                       GOVERN & ACT
                            │
                   Policies / Agents
                   Authority / Change
                            │
                            ↓
                   CONTINUOUS ENTERPRISE

That, in my view, is the point at which the CR sequence becomes a coherent platform architecture, rather than simply a sequence of metamodel enhancements.

The next major release should therefore be CR-11: Interoperability, Federation & Ecosystem Conformance, with particular attention to mapping OpenDEA to external EA/architecture standards and practical enterprise sources without compromising the canonical semantic model.
